## Pages & navigation

### Dashboard (`dashboard`)
The at-a-glance screen: sales totals, weight sold, material received, low-stock alerts and category charts.
Use the period chips (Today / This week / This month / Custom) to re-scope every tile and chart.
Tiles are clickable — they jump to the matching report with the same period applied.

### Products (`products`)
Your full inventory list with search, filters and per-row actions (Sell, Print label, Edit, Adjust stock, Delete).
Select rows with the checkboxes to open **Bulk edit** — change category, article, pricing mode, labour, stock or notes on many products at once.
**Generate barcodes** backfills missing EAN-13 codes; **Recalculate prices** freezes today's metal rate into every auto-priced product.
- **Add Product** — creates a product; SKU is generated by the shared core.
- **Print Label** — pick products, choose copies, print or download the label PDF.
- **CSV / Excel** — exports exactly the rows your filters currently show.

### Articles (`articles`)
Articles are the design/style line you group products by (e.g. 'Chandrahaar', 'Chain 22K'). Behaves exactly like Category.
The page lists every article with CRUD, plus analytics: stock value, units sold, weight sold, revenue and a 6-month trend.
Click an article to filter the Products page to it.

### Sales (`sales`)
The shop-counter screen: search or scan a product, set quantity and price, and complete a cash sale in seconds.
The history tab lists past sales with their payment status; delete a sale to restock the products it contained.

### Catalog (`catalog`)
Manage the option lists the product form offers: Colors, Sizes and Categories.
Rename an option to rename it everywhere; an option in use (productCount > 0) can be renamed but shows a warning before deletion.
You can also add a new value right from the product form's 'Custom…' option.

### Invoices / bills (`invoices`)
Every bill you've created, with status (Unpaid / Partial / Paid), customer, total and date.
Open a bill to view its lines, record a payment, edit it, re-print it in any template, or download the PDF.
Use the status chips and date filter to find a bill fast.

### Orders / quotes (`orders`)
A quote you save now and convert to a real bill later — the customer and line items are carried over.
Record part-payments against an order; its payment history appears in the order detail.
Convert to bill when the customer confirms; the order stays for your records.

### New Bill (`billing`)
The bill-creation screen: add items, set rate/weight/labour, discount, material returned and the amount paid.
The live estimate grid shows exactly what will print — gold and silver grids have their own column toggles.
Pick a template (E-commerce / Classic), optionally split the sheet 2-in-1, then Print or Download the PDF.

### Job Letters (`job-letter`)
Offer/job-work letters for karigars: what material was given, the agreed work, and its return date.
Print on letterhead; close a letter when the material comes back.

### Parties / Khata (`parties`)
Customers, suppliers and workers with running balances — the Khata ledger.
Open a party to see every transaction, record payments, and print a statement.
Balances feed the Advances page: whoever owes whom, in one list.

### Advances (`advances`)
Advances given to or taken from parties, with settle-up actions.
Settling an advance writes a matching ledger entry on the party's Khata.

### Reports (`reports`)
Seven report types with date ranges, totals, weight/material aggregates and CSV / Excel / PDF export.
The quick chips (Today / This week / This month / Custom) drive the date range.
See [Report types](#report-types) below for what each report answers.

### Settings (`settings`)
Everything shop-wide: profile, GST/address on bills, gold & silver rates, default labour, bill template, label printer, PIN lock and database backup.
Rates & labour is where the auto-price engine gets its numbers — the Help page's Pricing Guide explains the formula.

### Help (`help`)
The searchable Help Center (this content) plus the Pricing Guide (how gold/silver prices are computed).
Type in the search box to filter every entry; the link icon on any topic copies a deep link.

## New Bill — every field

### Customer
Who the bill is for. Optional for walk-in cash sales — leave blank and the bill prints 'Cash'.
Entering a phone that matches a party opens that party's Khata history alongside.

### Items (product line)
Each line is one product: search by name, or type/scan the barcode or SKU and press Enter.
Quantity multiplies the line; the line amount is computed by the shared core so every app prints the same bill.
A line can be an ad-hoc (non-catalogue) item — just type a name and price.

### Rate (₹/gm)
The metal rate used for this line. Auto-priced lines pull today's shop rate; you can override it per line.
The shop's current rates live in Settings → Rates & labour and print on the bill when the rate lines are ON.

### Weight
Line weight in grams (or the product's unit). For gold, the NET weight is what pricing uses — Chejat adds and Nag less subtracts to reach it.
Toggle 'Weight after name' (template option) to print the weight right after the product name instead of its own column.

### Labour
The karigar/making charge: Percent of the metal value, a Fixed ₹ amount, or Per gram.
Set a shop default in Settings; the product form can override it per product.

### Other charges & POVAYI
POVAYI and Other Charges are flat amounts added to the line total.
Both print as separate rows so the customer sees exactly what makes up the amount.

### Estimate grid columns
The printed estimate table. Gold grid columns: G. Wt., Chejat/Nag, Rate, Labour, Other Charges, Amount; silver grid uses its combined Weight column.
Turn columns off with the per-metal toggles on the New Bill screen — hidden columns are remembered per bill in the template settings.

### Discount
A flat ₹ discount applied after line totals — before material-returned adjustment.
Prints as its own 'Less: Discount' row.

### Material Returned
Old material the customer handed over: a title (e.g. 'Old Gold'), its weight and its agreed value.
The value is deducted from the bill total; the weight feeds the dashboard/report 'Material received' KPIs.

### Amount paid now
What the customer pays at the counter. Total − discount − material returned − paid = Due.
The live 'Paid ₹X · Due ₹Y' strip near the totals shows the result as you type.
₹0 paid leaves the invoice Unpaid; the full amount marks it Paid; anything in between is Partial.

### Payment method
How the money arrived: Cash, UPI, Card, Cheque, Bank transfer — or 'Custom…' for anything else (max 40 chars).
The method is stored on the payment record and shown in the bill/order payment history.

### Notes
Free text shown on the bill — warranty wording, return policy, anything.

### Template & Classic color
E-commerce is the shop default (clean estimate sheet); Classic is the traditional coloured receipt.
Classic color picks the accent (red/blue/green) used by the classic layout.

## Bill template toggles

### 2-in-1 (duplicate / distinct)
Prints two bills on one sheet. Duplicate = the same bill twice (customer copy + shop copy).
Distinct = a second, separate bill — pick it when two customers' items go on one sheet.

### Weight after name
ON: weight prints right after the product name. OFF: weight gets its own column (G. Wt.).

### Gold rate line
Prints the shop's gold rate (₹/gm) row above the estimate grid.

### Silver rate line
Prints the shop's silver rate (₹/gm) row — only relevant when silver items are on the bill.

### Purity (karat) line
The purity line under the gold rate: Base (shop's base karat), Product (the karat resolved from the bill's items), Custom text (e.g. '22K hallmark'), or Off.

### Chejat/Nag signs
ON: prints 'Chejat: 0.100 +' and 'Nag: 0.200 −' — showing chejat adds to net weight and nag less subtracts.
OFF: prints the plain values without + / −.

### Per-metal column toggles
Gold and silver grids keep their own hidden-column lists — turn off anything you don't print (e.g. Other Charges).
The setting is stored with the bill so a re-print always matches what the customer saw.

## Filters & search

### Products filters
Color / Size / Category / Article / Type narrow by attributes; Status filters by stock (In / Low / Out).
Pricing shows only Auto-priced or Manual-priced products. Clear removes every filter at once.
Filters survive navigation — jumping here from Catalog keeps the selection.

### Global search & barcode
The top search box matches name, SKU, barcode, color and article as you type.
The barcode input is for USB scanners: scan, press Enter, the product opens.
Camera scan uses the device camera for barcodes when no scanner is available.

### Reports date range
Quick chips set Today / This week / This month in one click; Custom opens the from/to dates.
The range drives the rows, totals and every export (CSV / Excel / PDF).

### Invoices status & date
Status chips (All / Unpaid / Partial / Paid) + date filter narrow the bill list.
Pair with search to find a bill by customer or number.

### Orders status filter
Open = quote still pending; Converted = already turned into a bill; the rest are closed/cancelled.

## Report types

| Report | Answers |
|---|---|
| **Daily Sales** | Today's (or a chosen day's) invoices: count, total, items sold and weight sold. |
| **Weekly Sales** | Day-by-day totals for the last 7 days — the quick 'is business picking up?' view. |
| **Monthly Sales** | This month's totals, with a per-day breakdown chart. |
| **Yearly Sales** | Month-by-month totals for the year — seasonality at a glance. |
| **Product Stock** | Every product's current stock and value — the full inventory position. |
| **Low Stock** | Products at or below their low-stock threshold, sorted worst-first — today's restock list. |
| **Sold Products** | What actually moved in a period: units, weight and revenue per product. |
